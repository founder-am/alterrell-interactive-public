## THESIS
The musicians crossed over. The sitcoms did something different.
Sitcom names reveal two mechanisms: character identity moving
a name (Whitley — the character's aspirational coding),
and actor fame moving a name (Alfonso — the person, not the role).
Counter-examples (Tisha, Tichina) show the pattern is not universal
and we did not cherry-pick.

## THREE-ACT STRUCTURE (locked)
Act 1: Living Single — ensemble legitimization
  Khadijah, Regine, Latifah moved together in one season.
  Muslim/Arabic names normalized through Black community TV.
Act 2: A Different World / Fresh Prince — character vs. actor
  Whitley (character identity) vs. Jasmine (confounded/Aladdin).
  Alfonso (actor beloved) vs. Carlton (character uncool).
  Kadeem (Arabic actor name) as supporting evidence.
Act 3: The counter-example
  Tisha Campbell, Tichina Arnold — Martin was huge,
  neither name moved. Why? Put to the reader.
  Interactive prompt: "Were you named after one of these?
  Tell us on Substack."
