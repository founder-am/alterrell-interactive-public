## THESIS (working, not locked)
Some names didn't need a single famous person.
They needed a community ready to claim something.
The Black Classics — Jamal, Malik, Darius, Kareem —
grew because of a cultural moment, not a celebrity moment.
Understanding which names belong in this category
and which don't is the research task.
