Key Findings

Green Flags

  • Strong alignment on company culture and values
  • Complementary skill sets with minimal overlap conflict
  • Both founders demonstrate high commitment levels
  • Healthy conflict resolution patterns observed in check-in responses

Red Flags

  • Exit Timeline Mismatch: F1 envisions a 10+ year journey; F2 targets 5-7 year exit. This fundamental difference requires explicit discussion and documented agreement.
  • Risk Tolerance Gap: During stress scenarios, founders showed divergent risk appetites that could cause friction during pivots.

Recommendations

  1. Schedule Exit Alignment Discussion: Before incorporating, have an explicit conversation about exit scenarios, timelines, and what "success" means to each founder.
  2. Establish Feedback Protocols: Create agreed-upon norms for how and when to deliver critical feedback to accommodate different processing styles.
  3. Document Decision Authority: Clearly define which decisions require consensus vs. individual authority to prevent future conflicts.
  4. Quarterly Check-ins: Schedule recurring partnership health checks using the 7 dimensions framework.
